Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 18, 2026

NotAStore ("we", "our", or "us") operates the NotAStore mobile application and the website at notastore.app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services.

Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive the following information from your Google account:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile picture (if available)
  • Google account identifier

We do not collect payment information. NotAStore is a browsing and wishlist app — no real purchases are made through the app.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Display your profile within the app
  • Keep you signed in across sessions
  • Improve and maintain our services
  • Respond to support requests

How we store your information

Your session and profile data are stored locally on your device. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Third-party services

We use Google Sign-In to authenticate users. Google's use of your information is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

Data retention

Your account data is retained on your device until you sign out or uninstall the app. You may request deletion of any server-side data we hold by contacting us.

Your rights

You may:

  • Sign out of the app at any time from your profile settings
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by emailing us
  • Revoke NotAStore's access via your Google Account settings

Children's privacy

NotAStore is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page and update the "Last updated" date above.

Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@notastore.app.
